## How can customer retention be measured?

You can measure your customer retention rate (CRR) with the help of the simple formula below, which takes into account the customers you have at the beginning (S), at the end (E), and any new customers you acquire throughout the period you're monitoring (N).

CRR = ((E-N)/S) x 100

Generally speaking, firms should strive for the highest possible customer retention rate. As a rule of thumb, most firms strive for a score of at least 85%.
